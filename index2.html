More details

Constantine is a supernatural action film released in 2005. The main character is John Constantine, played by Keanu Reeves. He is an exorcist who can see angels and demons and helps people fight supernatural forces.

The film is based on the DC Comics/Vertigo comic book Hellblazer. The story takes place in Los Angeles, where Constantine works with detective Angela Dodson to investigate the mysterious death of her twin sister. Their investigation leads them into a dangerous world of angels, demons, Heaven and Hell

Facts about the film
  • The film was directed by Francis Lawrence.
  • Constantine is based on the comic book Hellblazer.
  • This movie received an Oscar nomination for Best Visual Effects
